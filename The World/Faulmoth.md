# Faulmoth

**Location Type:** Metropolis  
**Nation:** The Council of the Circle  
**Population:** 80,000   
**Primary Industry:** Government, business/banking, import/export  
**Government:** Plutocracy (Rule by the wealthy). Officially overseen by the Council, but effectively managed by the Faulmoth Board of Trade.  
**Magic Level:** High
**Economic Level/Gold Limit:** Unlimited

**One-sentence description:** The port city of Faulmoth features a massive industrial port alongside a massive military port, but the city itself is run by wealthy bankers and businessmen -- implicitly and explicitly.  


**Unofficial Motto:** *Grow forever, never yield.*   
**Atmosphere:** Busy, opportunistic, cut-throat, anonymous

### At A Glance

Faulmoth is a sprawling, soot-stained metropolis that serves as the economic heartbeat of The Council of the Circle. It is a city of jarring contrasts: the rhythmic, industrial clatter of the docks grinds against the silent, velvet-draped halls of the banking district. While the military fleet provides a facade of rigid order, the city is actually governed by the ledger and the bribe.

## Racial Demographics
Faulmoth is a true melting pot where gold is the only language that matters. Most cultures and races live in a state of bustling, intermixed harmony. The exception to this rule is the elves -- few places in The Circle have persecuted elves as voraciously as Faulmoth. Many are hiding in sympathetic citizens' walls, cellars, in burrow-like pits dug in farmers' fields and parks, and in any precarious and overlooked spaces they can.

**The Kobold Quarter:**The city’s kobold population, long used as cheap labor for the narrowest tunnels of the industrial port, has reached a breaking point. They have no formal territory, but their presence in the Cistern District has turned from submissive to rebellious. Graffiti in Draconic warns of a "Great Unbinding."

## Key Districts
**The Twin Anchors:** Two massive ports side-by-side. To the west, the Industrial Port is a forest of cranes and steam-whistles. To the east, the Military Port houses the iron-clad warships of the Council.

**The Vaults:** The upper city. Marble buildings with gold-leaf trim. This is the home of the bankers and merchant princes who buy and sell nations over tea.

**The Low Tide:** The sprawling slums and markets surrounding the industrial port.

## Notable Feature: The Shadow Ledger (Thieves’ Guild)
Faulmoth’s Thieves’ Guild is one of the most active and organized in the world, but they rarely pick-pocket commoners or rob houses.

 The Guild is essentially a freelance paramilitary arm for the city’s elite. Powerful merchants "buy off" the Guild to conduct corporate sabotage. If a rival’s warehouse burns down or a shipment of silk is "lost" at sea, it was likely a paid Guild hit. The guild is so integrated into the economy that business losses to theft are often factored into annual banking projections.

 ## Active Threats
**The Soot-Wing Swarms:** A massive, unnatural population of bats plagues the city. They claim a 5-mile territory centered on the Cathedral of Coins. At dusk, the sky turns black with leathery wings. They are aggressive, carry "Blight-Fever," and have been known to carry off small pets or even infants from balconies.

**The Kobold Uprising:** Tensions are boiling over in the industrial sectors. Small-scale sabotage—clogged pipes, loosened bolts on cranes, and "accidental" fires—is occurring daily. The city guard is hesitant to enter the cramped crawlspaces where the kobolds congregate, fearing a coordinated ambush.
