# Come-By-Chance

**Location Type:** Thorp
**Nation:** The Islands of Rockchester   
**Population:** About 20 
**Primary Industry:** None/foraging
   
**Unofficial Motto:** *We live with the land.*   

### At A Glance

A town with 3 homesteads and a small shrine that acts as a meeting place when the families aren’t gathered around each others’ kitchen tables. There are eight buildings, although three of them haven’t been set foot in for years because they’re decrepit death traps. 

This thorp boasts one tanner, but no other services or craftsmen, although the people are warm and welcoming enough that most anyone will agree to try their hand at anything once. They just might not be any good at it, having never tried it before.
