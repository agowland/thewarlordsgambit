# Brookdale

**Location Type:** Village  
**Nation:** The Islands of Rockchester   
**Population:** About 500   
**Primary Industry:** Agriculture
   
**Unofficial Motto:** *The land provides all that we need.*  

**Government:** Hereditary Mayor  
**Magic Level:** High Magic -- 10% of the population are spellcasters and magic is an everyday technology that powers light sources, grants healing, allows for closer bonds between townsfolk and nature, and so on  
**Economic Level/Gold Limit:** 100 gp  
**Available Services:** Basic crafts, 1<sup>st</sup> and 2<sup>nd</sup> level spells
