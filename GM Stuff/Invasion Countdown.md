
## Circle Takeover Countdown

| **Order** | **Event**  |
|---|---|
|  9 | A team of covert Circle operatives arrive in Rockchester to start sowing dissent, especially in cities and areas where Elven refugees have congregated  |
| 8 |  The Council of the Circle renames itself -- the party encounters newspapers and town criers announcing that the Council of the Circle is now the Circle of Empire; the Chancellor is now the Emperor |
| 7|  A palace in a minor kingdom in the circle is destroyed with high casualties; Elves are blamed and several are framed and executed |
| 6|  The covert Circle operatives undertake acts of sabotage at the ports of Badgerbrook and West Bridge, allowing the Circle's armed forces to land on Rockchester and begin the invasion  |
| 5|  Workers at mines and natural resource camps throughout Rockchester begin to be harassed by Circle soldiers and officials; demands are made for the resources to be turned over to the Circle |
|4|  King Molloy issues a command requiring all residents of fighting age and ability to be conscripted into the King's Guard; those who are too old or too young, or with disabilities, should organize themselves into militias and form plans for how they will defend their homes and villages  |
|3 | The Circle's invasion becomes an all-out war, with coordinated strikes aimed at physically dividing Rockchester's defenders and impacting movement and logistics  |
|2 |  The Circle makes a final attempt at manipulating Rockchesterites, this time focusing on the armed forces and militias in an attempt to cause mutiny or abandonment of the cause |
|1 |  Attacks on supply lines and the successful blockade of Rockchester's ports means starvation is rampant; the party takes a point of stress per long rest unless something is done to change the situation |
| 0 |  The Warlord, Farley, is installed as Almighty Emperor over the Islands of Rockchester |
