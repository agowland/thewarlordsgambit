This section helps guide you (or your players, if you're running this campaign frame as a DM for your own table) through the onboarding process for *The Warlord's Gambit* campaign. It includes important resources, questionnaires, and reference materials to ensure everyone is prepared and aligned before diving into the adventure. It's also got resouces like [[LINK TO QUICK REFERENCE]] that you can jump back to over and over again when "What does Evasion mean, again?" or "How do I roll with Advantage?" questions pop into your mind and you don't want to rifle through the whole Core Rulebook. Please note that, because *The Warlord's Gambit* is homebrewed, **only** the resources in this section, other than the Character Questionnaire, are made to provide information that is not specific to this campaign.
