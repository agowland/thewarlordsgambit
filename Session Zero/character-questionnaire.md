## Character Questionnaire

---- 

This survey is meant to help me in making room in the world and story for your character, in ways that are deeply meaningful and, hopefully, surprising to the other players. It’s also got a secondary purpose – to help you consider your characters and their motivations more deeply, so that they become living, breathing beings whose lives you can more easily step into. Answer the questions from your character’s perspective – as if you’re them for the time it takes you to finish the survey (which can be as long or short of a time as you like!).

Please shoot your answers back to me so I can integrate it into a special scene for your character next time we meet!  aidan.gowland@gmail.com 

#### The Basics (Answer all of these)

- What is your name?
- How old are you? Is this young, middle-aged or elderly for your species?
- Where are you from? This can be very different from where you were born, but should be where you feel like you ‘grew up’ or were raised.
- Where do you call home? (This can be very different from your answer to where you’re from!)
- Where have you lived? If you’ve stayed mostly in one place, why? If you’ve lived in several places, why? What drew you to those places?
- How would you describe your appearance?

#### The Deeps (Choose 1-3 to answer, or more if you get super invested…)

- You once witnessed a profound injustice, when you were too young to do anything about it. What was the injustice? What do you wish, now, that you had done? (One person’s justice can be another person’s injustice! Maybe lawmen came and arrested your uncle for stealing and you see that as an injustice; maybe someone stole from your uncle and lawmen never came)
- Who has most influenced your life?
- Who have you always looked up to? Why? What about them was admirable? What do you wish to emulate?
- Until now, what is the moment in your life that you are most proud of?
- What is the moment you are most ashamed of? A decision was involved – to do something, or not to do something, perhaps – that changed everything. What decision did you make, and why does it continue to make you feel shame when you think of it?
- At some point in your life, you had to leave someone you love(d) behind. Either to move away, or because your morals and ethics diverged, or perhaps they passed out of the mortal realm. Who were they? What do you hope they’re doing now? What do you fear has happened to them?
- You once had a meal that made you feel like everything in life was perfect, and nothing could go wrong. The food might not have been perfect, but maybe it was about the people you were with and the mood of the table. Or maybe it was the worst group of people you can imagine, but the food was absolutely divine. Describe the meal. What was the context? What were you eating?
- Has your life turned out differently than you hoped or thought it would when you were a kid? In what way(s)?
- What is your biggest fear?
- What is your deepest hope?
- What needs protecting more than anything else in the world?
- What, in the whole world, needs to be extinguished? It can be an actual ‘thing’ (like ‘Yetis’) or something conceptual (like ‘capitalism’ or ‘hate’ or ‘love’ or ‘peace’). 
- If you weren’t doing what you’re doing now, what would you be doing?
- What does the perfect vacation look like?
- What does the perfect ‘day off’ look like?
- What possession would it break you to lose?
- There’s one object you’ve desired since you were a child, checking every market in case someone’s selling one. What is it?
- What regret keeps you awake at night? Why?
- How did the worst mistake you’ve ever made change you?
- If someone approached you and offered to remove a prominent scar from your body painlessly and for free, would you take them up on it? Why, or why not? 
- There’s a memory from your past that still haunts you. Describe it. What do you see? Smell? Hear? Feel?
- What act of generosity made a big impact on your life?
- You suffered a life-altering injury. What was it? How did you persevere? How does it affect you now?
