
Abraham needs a package delivered to a mate in Canter as soon as possible, before his mate’s ship sails. The package doesn’t weigh much – about a pound, which makes sense when the man explains that it’s a book he wants his friend to be able to enjoy on his voyage. He’ll pay *3 gold bags if the party delivers it right away, but it’ll only be 1 gold bag if they do anything other than rest before delivering it to Canter*

**SECRET**
The book is actually a notebook – handwritten instructions for a spell that will create the illusion of a massive fleet of Circle ships off the coast of Canter, drawing the attention of the King’s naval ships and creating a distraction that allows The Circle to attack the south of the Island, near [[Red Cliff]].

**TIE-IN QUESTS**
- The sailor [[Ronan Duffy]]is looking for knows that The Circle plans to use this spell, and can warn the party about it if they complete that quest before this one. If they complete this quest before the [[Ronan Duffy]] one, that one informs them of the deception they’ve fallen for here and their role in creating the distraction.
