- Captain of *The Busty Mermaid*, which is shoreside, waiting for a new mast he’ll likely never be able to afford.
- Was in [[The Griffin’s Roost]] and mentioned needing a hand with some seaweed for a new kind of lure, and is willing to pay *2 bags of gold* for good help

Declan tells the party that he needs a few extra hands heading out to The Bluffs, a mist-shrouded, steep and dangerous part of the coast about an hour’s hike from [[RPGs/1. Rulesets/Daggerheart/Worlds/Rockchester/Anchor Point|Anchor Point]]. There’s a bioluminescent seaweed that grows there – and only there, that he’s ever heard of – that he thinks will make a fantastic new lure that he can sell to other fishermen in order to earn the money to get his own ship back on the water.

**SECRET**
The seaweed is actually a byproduct of The Circle’s arcane naval engines, and Declan’s been collecting it and selling it back to them for months. Now that the weather’s turning, the job’s getting more dangerous, and The Circle is asking for more of it than he can collect on his own. Collecting the seaweed with Declan will see the party unknowingly help The Circle.
