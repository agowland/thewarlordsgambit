A Daggerheart Campaign, Designed & GM’d by Aidan Gowland

##### THE PITCH

*Alternate pitch:*
The Islands of Rockchester, nestled a two days’ rough sail from The Circle, have always been a desolate and forbidding place. Its people are welcoming, kind, and hardworking, forced to make their lives on a land that refuses to be tamed. It would reject them, surely, but that those people are its only protectors. The people, too, might have left, except there’s something enchanting about a place that clings to you as tightly as you cling to it, and an honour in getting to call such a place home. What the lands and waters around Rockchester can’t provide, they get from The Circle, sending ore and fish and timber back on Circle ships on their return. It’s a good arrangement. A safe arrangement. And though Rockchester has always vehemently declined becoming a member nation of The Circle, their relationship has been a good one. 

________

The islands of Rockchester sit a two-days’ journey away from The Circle, a global center of trade and power. These islands, however, are speckled with quaint, quiet hamlets and villages, full of folk enduring the realities of lives lived close with the land and inclimate weather, as they and their ancestors always have. Most folk don’t have much, though they’re proud of what they do have and are happy to share. But they also, collectively, protect a centuries-old secret – one that The Circle will stop at nothing to unearth. In *The Warlord’s Gambit*, your heroes will find themselves quickly caught up in a race to decide – and save – what, and who, matters most to them.
