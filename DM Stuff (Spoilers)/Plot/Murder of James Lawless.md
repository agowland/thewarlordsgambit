It is my unenviable, and angering, duty to inform you that James Lawless has been found dead in his cabin just a few minutes ago. We’re about a day’s sail from Badgerbrook, but I’m afraid no one will be permitted to go ashore until the matter has been resolved. For those who are due to connect with other ports, we will do our very best to arrange alternate transportation once we’ve docked. In the meantime, we’ll be dropping anchor and waiting for one of the King’s fleet to meet us to conduct an investigation.”

### FACTS
- James Lawless was poisoned with a quick-acting poison that had been hidden in his tea – it could easily have been altered in the kitchen, or on its way to him, or by another passenger he may have been entertaining in his cabin. 
- Owen Savage (deckhand) was in the ship’s company quarters with a rope
- Pete Beele (cook) was in the galley with the poison
- Thomas Cleaver (1<sup>st</sup> class cabin boy) was in the 3<sup>rd</sup> class births with a knife
- Owen Savage had made threats against Lawless over gambling debts Lawless had accrued by betting on the on-board fights, but had yet to pay
- Pete Beele had also made threats against Lawless, whom he believed had thrown a fight he was participating in, and which Beele had bet on
- Thomas Cleaver hadn’t made any threats, but had been reprimanded by Captain Holly for his insubordinate attitude toward Lawless. Cleaver had said that he’d show Lawless respect when he began treating him as a human instead of a mindless husk.

### CLUES

#### Julia Moran (Maid; about 16 years old; soft face, delicate build, half-elf, frizzy red hair; eager and sociable)
- “Mister Lawless wasn’t very nice to the crew, at least not the lower ranks of us. Yesterday Captain Holly told Tommy – the cabin boy – off for not being respectful enough to Mister Lawless, but Mister Lawless never said nothin’ to us that was worthy of any respect. And I know he owed money to Mister Savage over the fight nights; I heard Mister Savage cursin’ him out fierce in the crew quarters a couple of nights ago. And Cook lost a lot of coin betting on him in one of the fights, too, and kept insisting that he’d thrown it on purpose in order to lower what he owed to Mister Savage. Honestly, having Mister Lawless on board has been the most drama we’ve had in months.”

#### Owen Savage, deckhand (early 40s; angular face, statuesque build, human, shaved head; intense and efficient)
- "I had my differences with Lawless, sure, but the gambling debts were a thing of the past. He settled up with me yesterday and we were square. I was helping the First Mate re-stock a sail in the **ship’s storage room** below deck, and we were there for most of the morning. After that, I was just hanging out in the crew quarters, playing cards, until the bells started and Archie ran in and told us all about the murder.”

#### Pete Beele (mid-fifties, walks with a limp; square face, burly build, human, cropped hair; fidgety and suspicious)
- "I was in the **galley**, clearing up the dishes from lunch and getting ready to prep for supper. Cleaver came and told me Lawless wanted tea, so I stopped what I was doing and made some for him – two sugars and a pinch of sylvanus, as always. Thomas picked it up, and I saw him head straight for the captain’s quarters. The captain keeps a few of his own bottles and remedies for personal use in a locked cabinet in his quarters—nothing for the crew, mind you. I'm telling you, I didn't touch anything in there."

#### Thomas Cleaver (1<sup>st</sup> Class Cabin Boy, 12 years old; uneven face, emaciated build, human, curly hair; earnest and desperate)
- "I was down in the **3rd class berths** briefly. There was a young passenger who was feeling ill, and the Captain asked me to fetch a painkiller from his cabin; gave me the key an’ everyfin’. The Captain’s orders take priority. On my way back, Cook said he had tea that had to go to Mister Lawless. I swear I never went near the galley before taking the tea to Lawless. Lawless and I had our disagreements, but I would never harm a man for a few harsh words, and my whole family’s relyin’ on these wages."

#### Henry McDaniel (Mate)
- "I saw Owen Savage earlier this morning. He was in the **ship’s storage room** helping the First Mate, just as he said. After that, I saw him go to the crew quarters. He was messing with a length of rope. I remember there were some red stains on it as he was coiling it up."

#### Silas Vance (3<sup>rd</sup> Class Passenger)
- "I was feeling a little seasick after breakfast and went down to the **3rd class berths** for some quiet. I saw Thomas Cleaver down there. He wasn’t looking for a book, or fetching medicine. He was rummaging through a locker that didn't belong to him. When he saw me, he froze, and then he quickly walked away with a strange, secretive look on his face. I didn’t see what he was holding, but his hand was wrapped tightly around a small, square package."

#### Eleanor FInch (1<sup>st</sup> Class Passenger)
- "I saw something strange last night, but not this morning. I was walking past the galley and saw **Pete Beele**. He had a key in his hand, and I'm sure of it. He quickly put it in his pocket and walked away from the captain’s quarters. He looked a little nervous. He was carrying a small container of what looked like **dried, ground herbs**, which I thought nothing of at the time. I figured it was for a recipe."
