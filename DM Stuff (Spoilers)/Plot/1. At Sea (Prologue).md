# Overview

We join the party when they’re a few days into their sea voyage, coming from the somewhat distant port of Faulmoth, on [[The Circle]]. There are plenty of activities to keep the passengers of [[The Restless]] busy – scheduled fights they can both participate in and bet on, meals of varying quality depending on their budget, fishing attempts off the side of the ship or out on small dinghies when the wind’s died down or when they overnight in sheltered harbours, etc.

*Activities* can allow the party members to level up – e.g. when they ‘fish’, they’ll be rolling to determine their success, as with when they step into the ring to face-off against other passengers and crew. 

*Encounters* also allow the party to level up, but they won’t know they’re coming. A random encounter table will be rolled against periodically, either at random based on the DM’s discretion, or by making use of a character’s particular skill stat to determine whether an encounter is avoided.

It will take three days to get to the shores of Rockchester. Each activity the party chooses to engage in – or each encounter they stumble into – counts as one day.

- Subtly hint at things happening in Rockchester
	- Pass ships from The Circle’s navy, all heading in the same direction, with the number of ships growing as the party gets closer to Rockchester

# At The Table

> [!quote]
> A home for the hopeful and the desperate, _The Restless_ is a large, stout ship built not for speed or elegance, but for enduring the unforgiving seas. Her three masts rise like a weathered forest, each sail a patchwork of canvas, mended and patched so many times that the original color is a distant memory. The hull, painted a faded gray, is scarred with the scuffs and scrapes of a thousand journeys, its timbers groaning and sighing with every swell. On deck, the air is thick with the practical smells of pitch, salt, and wet wool, and the constant, rhythmic creak of the rigging is the ship's own heartbeat. Though she lacks the magical grace of the vessels that cost twice as much, her formidable size and sturdy construction tell a story of resilience—a vessel that carries its burdens with a slow, relentless patience, a mirror to the people she carries toward a new life.



> [!encounter]
> A sudden, unnatural quiet falls over the water as a sleek, silver-hulled vessel cuts through the waves, its prow slicing the water with almost no sound. There's no sail on the mast, and no crew at the oars; the ship is driven forward by an arcane engine that hums with a faint, crystalline glow. The ship is a naval patrol vessel, clearly commissioned by a state with deep coffers. It pulls alongside _The Restless_ with impossible grace, its hull bearing the symbol of the Council of the Circle—a stylized compass rose with a single, burning eye at its center.
> 
> A figure on the deck, dressed in a crisp uniform and an elaborate naval cap, raises a speaking horn. "By authority of the Council of the Circle," a low, amplified voice rings out across the water, "we demand to board and inspect your vessel under the provisions of the Maritime Governance Act."
> 
> The claim hangs in the air like a challenge, as both you and your captain know that the Council's laws don't hold sway in these open waters. Their presence here is a bold, illegal move, and the reason for their interest in _The Restless_ is a mystery.
