# Act 2: The Adventure
This is the longest act, where the heroes’ journeys truly unfold.

#### Break Into Two
The party makes a firm decision. They cross the threshold from their old world into the new, committing fully to the main quest.

#### B Story
A secondary plotline is introduced, often involving a new character or relationship that helps reinforce the campaign's theme. For _Daggerheart_, this could be a side quest that runs alongside the main plot.

> [!attention] 
> **Side Quest:** Describe the side quest.

#### Fun & Games
The "promise of the premise." This section is where the players get to explore the new world, use their abilities, and have a good time. It's all about fulfilling the fantasy of the game, with moments of heroic success and discovery.

#### Midpoint
A major twist or turning point. The party either achieves a **false victory** (they think they've won, but things are about to get much worse) or a **false defeat** (they think they've lost, but a new opportunity appears). The stakes are raised and the campaign's direction shifts dramatically.

> [!failure] 
> **What do they think they’ve won? How will things get worse?:** Answer here.
> *or*
> **What do they think they’ve lost? What opportunity do they stumble upon?:** Answer here.


#### Bad Guys Close In
The heroes' opposition regroups and escalates. The "bad guys" (which can be a villain, a faction, or even a natural force) become more aggressive and pose a more direct threat. The party might face new internal or external conflicts.

> [!warning] 
> **What do the bad guys gain (alliances, weapon, resources, people, territory) that changes the nature of their threat or makes them a more serious threat to the party?:** Answer here.
>
 **What new internal or external conflict(s) does the party face?:** Answer here.

#### All Is Lost
The absolute lowest point of the campaign. The heroes suffer a devastating setback, and something or someone important "dies"—this could be a key NPC, a cherished item, or even the hope of a quick victory. The campaign's original goal feels impossible to achieve.

# Act 3: The Resolution
The heroes, changed by their journey, confront their final challenge.

#### Dark Night of the Soul
The heroes grieve their loss and process their failure. This is a period of reflection where they realize they need to change their approach to succeed. It’s a quiet moment before the final push.

#### Break Into Three
The heroes, armed with new understanding, figure out a new plan of attack. They "synthesize" what they've learned in the campaign and commit to their final assault.

#### Finale
The climax. The heroes confront the main antagonist or resolve the central conflict in a series of escalating scenes. This is where they use everything they've learned and show how much they've grown.

#### Final Image
A mirror of the Opening Image, showing the "after." It's a final snapshot of the heroes and their world, demonstrating how everything has been transformed by their adventure.
