Nation: The Circle

A two-day sea voyage (in good weather, and without any complications) from [[The Islands of Rockchester]], The Circle is a large, only vaguely circular continent that straddles the northernmost pole and stretches down to about halfway to the planet’s equator. The Circle is a *sui generis* supranational organization that shares characteristics of both confederations and federations and represents sixteen member nations. While the member nations draft and enforce many regulations and pieces of legislation within their territories, their own national laws cannot go against the statutes legislated by The Council of The Circle. Each nation is also responsible for ensuring that they enforce The Circle’s legislation and for contributing finances, materiel, personnel and some portions of their territories for the activities of The Circle, such as bureaucratic and military activities. The Council of the Circle has worked hard to ensure that all elements of supranational governance remain equally distributed amongst the member nations, so that no one nation gains an advantage in any one area – e.g. no nation produces more proficient bureaucrats, diplomats, sailors or soldiers than any other.
