## What I hope to do as a DM
- Co-create a cinematic story that lets us move through the wide range of experiences and emotions that tell us what it means to be human
- Be the party’s biggest cheerleader
- Be the facilitator of your stories and a world for you to explore, not the narrator of my own story that you get to play in

## What I need from players
- Be honest if there’s something missing from the story or something I could do that would be more enjoyable for you or better integrate your character into the story and world
- Think of ways that your character fits into the world, and seek avenues of connection (e.g. think up NPCs they may know in other cities, or places they may have travelled to as a child, etc.)
- Cheerlead your fellow players and, if the spotlight’s on them, pay attention to their story (e.g. rather than looking at your phone or reading the rulebook, etc.)
- Let yourself, and your character, make mistakes – we all will!

## Safety Tools
- Send me a message privately if there are themes, scenarios, names or anything else you’d prefer we avoid (you don’t have to explain why!). If that theme is central to the storyline, or may be central to a specific session, in a way that’s not avoidable, I’ll let you know in advance so we can come up with a game plan (e.g. if it’s central to the story, let’s work together to come up with a kickass story you’d like to play in the future; if it’s central to a session, let’s talk about what your character will do during the session and you can rejoin us the following session). 
- Please avoid using **red dice** as your duality dice. Each person will get a red d12 as a visual (but subtle) indicator that they need the story to go in a different direction, etc. As soon as I see a red die, I’ll invite us all to take a quick bathroom break and we can figure out what the issue is and what we can do to make it more comfortable. If you see someone rolling with a red die and I don’t notice it, please suggest that we take a quick bathroom break!
