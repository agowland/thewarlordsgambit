> > These dice are the least popular dice among fraudulent players, as the cheating aspect of the dice is easy to spot. High dice have had the dice faces of 1, 2, and 3 replaced with the numbers 4, 5, and 6. While playing dice games using these dice, you can’t roll 1s, 2s, or 3s. If someone suspects something is off with the dice, they must succeed on a DC 5 Investigation check to determine that the dice have been tampered with.
