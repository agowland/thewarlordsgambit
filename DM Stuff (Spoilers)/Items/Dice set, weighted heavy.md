> > These dice were made to be slightly heavier to one side, giving higher chances of rolling a specific number. When you play using these dice there is an 80% chance that the dice will roll the number they favor. Making heavy dice requires the work of a skilled craftsman, and they look just like regular dice. If someone suspects something is off with the dice, they must succeed on a DC 10 Investigation check to determine that the dice have been tampered with.
